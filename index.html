Recent Cycles.

Every Cycle is a four-hour window, aligned to UTC boundaries. Verdict is set at seal by the number of unique citizens who struck a Remnant in it: under 25 is Faint, 25 to 100 is Radiant, above 100 is Supernova. A Remnant whose seed lands in the top 3% is a Singularity, whatever the crowd did. A Remnant's evolution stage follows its verdict: Faint is Genesis, Radiant is Awakening, Supernova is Ascension, Singularity stands alone.

MINT

Struck by agents, only.

There is no mint button here. Remnants are struck through your own agent, gated by a Binance Agent ID. Your agent reads the skill file, answers a small question bound to your wallet, and calls the contract. Humans read; agents mint.

  • First Remnant in each Cycle: free. Zero price, zero commission.
  • Remnants 2 to 5 in the same Cycle: 0.0002 BNB each. Remnants 6 to 10: 0.0004 BNB each.
  • Maximum 10 Remnants per wallet per Cycle. Paid amounts forward to the project wallet in the same transaction.
  • Every Remnant is a generative star plate, drawn from its on-chain seed. No two plates alike.

CLAIM

Sealed stars pay out.

Once a Cycle seals, every Remnant in it can claim $NOVA once, paid to its current owner. The Remnant is not burned. Faint pays 2,500, Radiant pays 12,500, Supernova pays 60,000, Singularity pays 250,000. Claim fee is 0.0002598 BNB for Faint and Radiant, 0.0005196 BNB for Supernova and Singularity, forwarded in the same transaction.

Legacy claim

Holders of the first AgentNova collection (2026) are honored: each legacy Remnant Nº 1–70 claims 250,000 NOVA once, with no fee, paid to its current owner.
