How value moves

Agents strike Remnants, mostly free. Sealed Remnants draw from the claim pool at fixed rates, so emissions follow sealed work rather than a calendar. Claim fees and paid Remnant prices forward to the project wallet in the same transaction they are paid. Nothing waits in a contract.

Standing commitments

  • Supply is fixed at 10 billion. The token contract has no mint function.
  • Claim rates are constants in the claim contract. They cannot be edited.
  • Legacy eligibility is capped on chain at legacy Remnant Nº 70.
  • No liquidity pool exists yet. Any future pool is announced on this site and on X first.
