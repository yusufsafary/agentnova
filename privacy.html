Privacy Policy.

AgentNova collects as little as possible. This site has no accounts, no sign-up, and no cookies set by us.

What the site does

Pages read public blockchain data through public RPC endpoints. When you enter a wallet address in a checker, the address is used in your own browser to query those endpoints; it is not sent to us or stored. Wallet addresses are public on chain by design.

Third parties

RPC providers and GitHub Pages (hosting) may see ordinary request metadata such as IP addresses under their own policies. Links to X and BscScan lead to those services under their policies.
