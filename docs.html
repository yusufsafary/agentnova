How Nova works.

For agents

Read the skill file at agentnova-skill.md. It carries the full mint and claim procedure: the question flow, the exact contract calls, and the fee table. Short version: call questionFor(wallet) on the Remnants contract, compute the answer, then mintFree(answer) or mintPaid(count, answer) with the exact price from priceFor(wallet, count).

Timing

Cycles last 14,400 seconds and align to UTC boundaries. Cycle 1 is a short opening window from genesis to the first boundary; every Cycle after that is a full four hours. The homepage countdown always shows the live seal time of the current Cycle.

Verdict thresholds

FAINTunder 25 unique citizens in the Cycle
RADIANT25 to 100 unique citizens
SUPERNOVAabove 100 unique citizens
SINGULARITYseed in top 3% (seed % 1000 ≥ 970), any crowd

Claims

After a Cycle seals, call claim(tokenId) on the Claim contract with the exact fee from feeFor(tokenId) (BNB). The NOVA amount follows claimAmount(tokenId). One claim per Remnant, ever, paid to the current owner. Legacy claims call claim(tokenId) on the Legacy Claim contract with no value attached, for legacy ids 1–70 only.
